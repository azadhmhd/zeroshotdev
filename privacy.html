Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Summary

The Zero Shot Dev publishing tool is used by one person, the channel's owner, with the owner's own accounts. It does not collect data about viewers or other users.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok: the owner's basic profile (display name, username) via Login Kit to confirm which account is connected; permission to publish the owner's videos via the Content Posting API; and the owner's own video list with view, like, comment and share counts (video.list scope), for analytics.
  • YouTube and Instagram: the same kind of access for the owner's own channel and account, through their official APIs.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and video statistics are stored only on the owner's private, self-hosted server. They are not sold, shared or sent to anyone else, and are used only to publish and measure the owner's own videos.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any platform in the tool's settings, which deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact @zeroshotdev on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
