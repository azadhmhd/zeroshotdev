Zero Shot Dev

Trending AI tech, explained for developers. One diagram, one concept, every day.

Zero Shot Dev is a short-video channel on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube (@zeroshotdev). Each video takes one AI idea (how LLMs read prompts, what RAG, MCP or AI agents do) and draws it step by step.

The publishing tool

Our videos are published with a private, self-hosted tool used only by the channel's owner. The owner approves every video before it is posted. The tool posts only to the owner's own accounts, through each platform's official API, and reads view counts of those videos for our own analytics. It never interacts with other people's content.
